# Privacy Policy – SliverLake Calculator

**Effective Date:** 12 November 2026

SliverLake Calculator ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information Collection

SliverLake Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to external servers.

The App does not require users to create an account, register, or provide personal information.

## 2. How the App Works

All calculator operations are performed locally on your device.

The App does not require an internet connection to perform its core functions. Calculation inputs and results are processed on your device.

## 3. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or disclose users' personal information to third parties.

The App does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

The App does not maintain a cloud database or transmit calculation data to external servers.

Any device-level settings or preferences, if applicable, remain on your device.

## 5. Permissions

The App does not require access to sensitive device permissions to perform its calculator functions.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
